    Sources. Borders: Cliopatria v0.2.1 (Seshat Global History Databank), polity borders by the year to 2024, changed by VM: the shapes simplified for the web, and the rows that join other rows (such as the British Empire) left out; CC BY 4.0. States are joined across sources by their Wikidata items. Leaders to 2015 and how they took and left office: Archigos 4.1 (Goemans, Gleditsch and Chiozza), which states no licence; kept until VM’s owner decides. Leaders after 2015, heads of state and government, parties in power, national elections and referendums: Wikidata (CC0). Year pages and event lists: Wikipedia (CC BY-SA). Four elections are corrected where this site’s copy of Wikidata had the wrong date or country (Saint Vincent and the Grenadines 2025; Grenada 1967, 2003 and 2026): each takes its date and country from its English Wikipedia article, and they are listed, with their sources, in scripts/election-corrections.mjs in this site’s code.

    Method. Each state is coloured by the most significant thing that happened to it that year: an irregular seizure of power (a coup or other irregular entry in Archigos), otherwise a new leader, otherwise a change of borders. Borders are by the year, in Cliopatria’s sample years: each year is drawn on Cliopatria’s rows for that year, and a border change is a year in which a state’s area in Cliopatria differs from the year before’s by more than 1%. 2025 and 2026 are shown at 2024, the last year Cliopatria holds. A state is covered from its first leader to its last, and Cliopatria’s polity rows are matched to the covered states by name and year in scripts/cliopatria-states.mjs. Over a period of years, each state takes the most significant thing that happened in any of them, on the borders at the end of the period, and the panels list every leader, party and event of the period. Polities that are not covered states appear uncoloured.

    Limits. Cliopatria gives one border a year, so the border animation moves from year to year, and a change within a year cannot be shown. Where a covered state has no row of its own in Cliopatria for a year (San Marino and Liechtenstein in every year; Denmark from 1941 to 1944, which Cliopatria draws inside Nazi Germany; Poland from 1944 to 1952; Japan from 1946 to 1952), the page says: VM cannot place this on a free border for that year. Cliopatria’s rows also hold lands a state ruled but that were not part of it (Greenland with Denmark from 1946; Algeria, which has a row of its own, with France from 1958 to 2023), so a state’s area in Cliopatria can be far larger than the state. Archigos names some leaders by surname only; where Wikidata has no match, the leader links to a Wikipedia search. After 2015 each country follows the office Archigos tracked, so a strong leader in the other office appears under “Also in office”. Election coverage depends on Wikidata.
